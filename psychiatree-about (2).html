What We Stand For

A practice rooted in something deeper

PsychiaTree was built on the belief that mental wellness is not a destination — it is a living, growing process. Just as a tree draws strength from its roots to weather every season, we believe each person has an inner foundation that, when nurtured, supports resilience, connection, and a life well-lived.

We are committed to normalizing mental health — not as something reserved for crisis moments, but as an essential, everyday part of being human. Every one of us navigates struggles, doubts, and seasons of difficulty. At PsychiaTree, there is no judgment, no shame, and no minimum threshold of suffering required to deserve care. You are welcome here exactly as you are.

Our approach honors the full complexity of who you are — your mind, your body, your relationships, and your story. We combine the precision of evidence-based medicine with the warmth of genuine human connection, because we believe both are necessary for real healing.

Joanna's Story

Why this work matters

Joanna Sharp has spent more than a decade at the intersection of physical and mental health — and it is precisely that vantage point that shapes everything about the care she provides. As both a Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner and a Family Nurse Practitioner, she has always understood what too many healthcare systems still overlook: that the mind and body are not separate systems, and that treating one without the other leaves the most important parts of a person unaddressed.

Joanna's path into psychiatric care was guided by a conviction she has carried throughout her career — that understanding oneself is one of the most powerful tools a person can have. The way we see ourselves shapes the way we move through the world, the quality of our relationships, the choices we make, and the life we are able to build. When that self-understanding is clouded by unaddressed mental health struggles, everything becomes harder than it needs to be. When it is cultivated and supported, the possibilities for growth become extraordinary.

Her work is also deeply personal in its philosophy. Joanna believes — without reservation — that mental health is not a niche concern for a select few. It is a universal human experience. Every person, regardless of background, identity, or circumstance, navigates internal struggles. Every person deserves a space where those struggles can be named, understood, and worked through without shame. One of her greatest missions is to help dismantle the stigma that keeps so many people from seeking the care that could genuinely change their lives.

In her practice, Joanna brings the same energy to a new patient walking through the door for the first time as she does to someone she has cared for for years — curiosity, patience, and a genuine desire to understand not just what someone is experiencing, but who they are and what they most want for their life. She named her practice PsychiaTree because that metaphor captures something she deeply believes: that lasting wellness grows from the roots up, and that the most meaningful work in psychiatric care happens when we take the time to tend to what lies beneath the surface.

Clinical Philosophy

How Joanna approaches care

Every appointment is an opportunity to do something meaningful. Joanna's clinical approach combines the rigor of evidence-based medicine with the humanity of truly listening — because she knows that the most accurate diagnosis and the most effective treatment plan begin with understanding the whole person sitting across from her.

With over a decade of experience spanning both primary care and psychiatric medicine, she brings a uniquely integrated perspective that most providers cannot offer.

01
Listen before prescribing
Every person's story is different. Joanna takes the time to truly understand before making recommendations — because the right care starts with being heard.
02
Normalize, never minimize
Mental health struggles are human, not shameful. She creates a space where you can be honest about what you're going through without fear of judgment.
03
Integrate mind and body
As both a PMHNP and FNP, Joanna sees the full picture — how physical health, lifestyle, and mental wellness are deeply intertwined and must be addressed together.
04
Partner, don't prescribe
Your care plan is built with you, not for you. Joanna believes in collaborative treatment — where your goals, values, and preferences are always part of the conversation.
05
Growth over perfection
Healing is not a straight line. She celebrates every step forward and remains with you through every season — not just the easy ones.
